# Open a moderated home for help and playtesting

## Description

Choose a durable venue and a person responsible for moderation. Give beginners clear ways to ask questions, introduce projects and request feedback, plus conduct and reporting information.

## Acceptance Criteria

Verify: A guest can read help and playtest topics, a new member can sign in and post, and a moderator can receive a report and act on it. Record venue ownership and how to keep or export useful public history.
